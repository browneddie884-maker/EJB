How it works

FAQ

Before you book

How old do I need to be to rent?

The main driver must be at least 21 and have held a full licence for one year. Drivers aged 21–24 pay a young driver fee of $25 per day and can rent Economy through SUV.

What do I need to bring?

A valid driver's licence and a credit or debit card in the main driver's name. International visitors also need their passport.

What is the fuel policy?

Full-to-full. You get the car with a full tank and return it full. If it comes back short, we refuel it at the local pump price plus a $15 service fee.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Cancel for free up to 48 hours before pickup. After that, a one-day charge applies. You can change dates or car class at any time, subject to availability.

Can someone else drive?

Add extra drivers at the desk for $13 per day each. Spouses and domestic partners drive free.
